2. Hardware Setup & Integration Guide

Direct Hardware Drivers

Brand 4 Less includes native drivers for your store's physical equipment. No third-party software or bridge utilities are needed.

Label Printer

TSC Brand Label Printer

Models: TE200, TTP-244 Pro, DA210, TC200 (TSPL Engine)

1. Connect USB cable to Windows PC. Insert roll of 50mm x 30mm sticky clothing labels.
2. Calibration: Turn printer OFF. Hold FEED button and turn ON. Release when it feeds 2 labels. Solid green light = calibrated.
3. Print Labels: Go to Product Inventory ➔ Print Barcode Labels ➔ Click [Direct Print (TSC)].
4. Self-Test: In Settings ➔ Hardware ➔ Click [Test TSC Label Printer] to print a test sticker.
Receipt Printer

DTS POS Thermal Printer

Models: DTS POS-80, DTS POS-58, ESC/POS Standard

1. Connect USB to PC. Insert 80mm thermal paper roll (paper feeding from bottom).
2. Connect the RJ11 cable from your Cash Drawer into the printer port marked DK.
3. Checkout Print: In POS, click [Direct Print (DTS Thermal)]. Prints in <1 second, cuts paper, and opens drawer.
4. Self-Test: In Settings ➔ Hardware ➔ Click [Test DTS Receipt Printer] to test print and cash drawer pop.
Barcode Scanner

SpeedX Barcode Scanner (USB HID Keyboard Wedge)

Plug the SpeedX USB cable into any free USB port on your computer. Windows activates it immediately.

  • Zero-Click Global Detection: You do not need to click on any search bar. Point the scanner at any tag from anywhere on the POS screen and pull the trigger.
  • Instant Reaction: Within 20 milliseconds, the app sounds an audio confirmation BEEP, adds the item to the active cart, and displays a green confirmation banner: "SpeedX Scanned & Added: [Product Name]".
  • Live Scanner Diagnostic: In Settings ➔ Hardware, scan any item in the test input box to verify real-time decoding.

3. POS Billing Terminal & Customer Records

Counter Operations

Cashier Keyboard Shortcuts

Shortcut Action Description
F1 Focus Search Bar Selects search box for typing product name, color, or manual code
F4 Collect Payment Opens checkout payment window when items are in cart
F8 Hold Active Cart Saves current cart in memory when customer picks extra clothes
ESC Cancel / Close Closes active modal, receipt preview, or discount window

Step-by-Step Checkout Workflow

  • 1. Scan Items: Scan garment tags with the SpeedX Scanner or select product cards from the category grid.
  • 2. Customer Registration: Type the Customer's Name and Mobile Phone (e.g. Usman Tariq, 03001234567). If they are a new customer, their profile is automatically created and saved upon completing checkout!
  • 3. Discounts: Click "Add Discount" to apply a flat PKR amount or percentage deduction.
  • 4. Collect Payment: Press F4. Select Cash (system calculates change), Card, Bank Transfer (IBFT), Khata (Credit), or Split Payment.
  • 5. Thermal Receipt: Click "Confirm & Print Receipt" ➔ DTS thermal printer prints receipt and opens cash drawer.

Customer Records & Purchase History

Access via "Customer Records" in the left sidebar to view your store's customer database:

  • Lifetime Purchase Tracking: View total completed visits, total lifetime spend in PKR, and active Khata debt.
  • Historical Invoice Drawer: Click [History] on any customer to inspect all past sales bills.
  • 1-Click Receipt Reprint: Click [Reprint DTS Receipt] on any past bill to reprint an exact copy on your thermal printer.
  • Customer Khata Settlement: Record incoming customer credit payments, print signed vouchers, and export PDF/Excel statements.

4. Inventory, Expenses, P&L & Backups

Management

Product Inventory & Moving WAC Costing

  • Variant Matrix: Manage garment items with multiple sizes (S, M, L, XL, 30, 32, 34) and colors with individual barcodes.
  • Automatic Weighted Average Cost (WAC): When receiving new shipments at fluctuating prices, the system recalculates true stock valuation automatically: WAC = (Old Stock Cost + New Shipment Cost) / Total Quantity.
  • Barcode Sticker Printing: Generate 50x30mm retail price stickers with scannable Code-128 barcodes directly to your TSC printer.

Operating Expenses & Staff Salaries

  • Daily Petty Cash: Record shop expenses (tea, customer refreshments, generator fuel, shopping bags) with instant daily totals.
  • Monthly Overheads: Track major monthly expenses (Shop Rent, LESCO/K-Electric Bills, Internet) with billing month tracking.
  • Staff Payroll Register: Register cashiers and sales staff with monthly salaries. Click [Disburse Salary] at month-end to auto-post payout entries.

Financial Analytics & Profit/Loss Statement

  • Trading Profit Statement: Gross Sales − Customer Discounts = Net Revenue. Net Revenue − Cost of Goods Sold (COGS at Moving WAC) = Gross Trading Profit.
  • Net Operating Profit: Gross Profit − Operating Expenses (Rent, Bills, Salaries, Petty Cash) = Net Take-Home Profit.
  • Stock Valuation: Complete audit of total capital invested in inventory at cost vs gross realizable retail value.
  • Excel Exports: Download official spreadsheet reports with one click.

Database Backups & Data Protection

  • 1-Second Hot Snapshot: Go to Settings ➔ Database Backup ➔ Click [Create Backup Now]. A complete transactional database snapshot is generated instantly.
  • USB Flash Drive Copy: Click [Download] on any backup snapshot to copy the database file to a USB flash drive for safe offline storage.
